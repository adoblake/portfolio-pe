About

A Product Engineer with a concept-art brain and a designer’s eye.

Hello! I’m Adonnis. I have a deep passion for art, a Bachelor’s Degree in Concept Art from Collins College, and a current focus on product engineering, UI/UX systems, Claude-assisted problem solving, and live product testing.

View work

I’m strongest when I can help turn a problem ticket, loose product idea, or unclear workflow into something people can understand, test, and build on.

Current focus

Product engineering at TurboVets.

At TurboVets, my focus has moved into Product Engineering. I’m working closer to the actual problem-solving side of the product: reading GitHub tickets, understanding the expected behavior, using Claude to reason through solutions, and live-testing changes so the product behaves the way people need it to.

That still includes UI/UX thinking, flows, hierarchy, and feature clarity, but design is now the support system under the engineering work. My graphic design and concept art background helps me spot clarity issues, while LEAN principles help me keep the process structured and practical.

New adventure

Learning product engineering and AI-assisted building in public.

This portfolio is part of the learning process. It was not treated as a one-shot static design. It was built through rounds: layout critique, visual direction, project additions, modal interactions, image galleries, case-study structure, About page, inquiry flow, dark/light mode, and new positioning as my title shifted into Product Engineer.

The process mirrors how I’m approaching this new chapter: read the problem, research the system, use AI to accelerate the solve, test the result, simplify the experience, and keep moving. I’m continuously searching for better education around product engineering, GitHub workflows, UI/UX, AI tooling, and how creative people can use these tools without losing their own style.

How I work

Product first. Design clarity always.

Art is still at the heart of how I see things, but my current work is increasingly about whether the product functions, whether the logic makes sense, and whether the user can complete the task without friction. I love the challenge of combining build logic with design clarity.

I thrive in team environments because good work gets sharper through conversation, tickets, testing, review, and iteration. My goal is to keep evolving: learn the engineering side deeply, use AI responsibly, and build work that feels clear, useful, and human.
