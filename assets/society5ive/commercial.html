Commercial Design

Functional design that works beautifully.

Commercial interiors should support the business, the brand, and the people who use the space every day. Society 5IVE designs offices, hospitality environments, and boutique commercial spaces with flow, durability, comfort, and experience in mind.

Start Your Project

Commercial interiors shaped around purpose, flow, and feeling.

Every project begins by understanding what the space needs to accomplish and how people should feel inside it.

The design direction is then built through planning, materials, furnishings, finishes, and details that support the larger experience.

Our Process

From lifestyle to layout.

  1. DiscoveryWe begin with conversation, goals, routines, constraints, and the feeling the space needs to create.
  2. ConceptWe translate those insights into layouts, palettes, materials, furniture direction, and visual language.
  3. ImplementationWe coordinate details, vendor communication, selections, and the steps needed to bring the plan to life.
  4. CompletionWe style, refine, and hand over a space that feels complete, livable, and connected to its purpose.
