About Society 5IVE

Spaces crafted from connection.

Founded around a more human approach to interior design, Society 5IVE creates homes and commercial spaces that feel personal, grounded, functional, and enduring.

Start Your Project

We ask better questions before we design better rooms.

Society 5IVE believes great interiors should reflect the people who live, work, gather, and restore inside them. That means slowing down, studying patterns, honoring practical needs, and building a design direction that feels true to the client.

The studio’s work blends warmth, craft, sustainable thinking, and timeless material choices so each space feels current now and considered years later.

Meet The Founder

Tasha Navejas

With a career shaped by curiosity, culture, and a respect for lasting craftsmanship, Tasha approaches interiors as a conversation between people, place, and purpose.

Her work balances historical reference, modern comfort, and environmentally conscious choices to create interiors rich with meaning and built for real life.

BS Interior Design, Northern Arizona University

Our Process

From lifestyle to layout.

  1. DiscoveryWe begin with conversation, goals, routines, constraints, and the feeling the space needs to create.
  2. ConceptWe translate those insights into layouts, palettes, materials, furniture direction, and visual language.
  3. ImplementationWe coordinate details, vendor communication, selections, and the steps needed to bring the plan to life.
  4. CompletionWe style, refine, and hand over a space that feels complete, livable, and connected to its purpose.
